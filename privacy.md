# Tnak for Rich House Realty - Privacy Policy

Tnak is an internal tool of the real estate agency Rich House Realty, used only by the agency's own staff.

## What the app accesses

With the permission of the agency's Google account (richhousereailty@gmail.com), the app uploads and reads property photos in the agency's own Google Drive and works with the agency's own Google Sheets with property listings and clients. This data is sent by the agency's staff through the agency's Telegram bot.

## What the app does not do

The app does not access data of any other Google users, does not sell or share data with third parties, and does not use it for advertising. Data stays in the agency's Google account and on the agency's server.
